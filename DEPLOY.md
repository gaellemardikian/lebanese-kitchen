# How to get this on your phone

## Option 1 — Netlify Drop (fastest, free, no CLI needed)

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser
2. Drag the entire **"website recipes"** folder onto the page
3. Wait ~30 seconds — you get a live URL like `https://random-name.netlify.app`
4. Open that URL on your phone
5. On iPhone: tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
   On Android: tap ⋮ menu → "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app"

The app works offline after first load (service worker caches everything).

## Option 2 — GitHub Pages (free, versioned)

1. Create a repo on github.com
2. Upload all files in this folder to the repo root
3. Go to Settings → Pages → Source: main branch, / (root)
4. Your URL: `https://yourusername.github.io/reponame`

## Option 3 — Vercel (free, also drag-and-drop)

1. Go to https://vercel.com/new
2. Click "Import" → drag folder → deploy

---

Once installed on your phone, the app icon appears on your home screen and works like a native app — even without internet.
